Heatmap variants

## v0 · current

Year in weeks, 5-step scale from quartiles of your own days, reading/listening toggle, tooltip is the native title.

daily reading
lessmore

## v1 · richer scale + tooltip

Same weeks layout, 7 steps on fixed minute thresholds (so a 90-minute day looks the same in any year), month labels with hours, best day outlined, and a hover/tap tooltip with minutes, pages and listening.

daily reading

## v2 · month blocks + streaks

One small calendar per month with day numbers, hours and pages in each month header, and streak markers: orange bar = longest streak, blue bar = current streak.

daily reading

## v3 · reading / listening split

Year in weeks again, but every day is two stacked halves: reading on top (green), listening below (purple). Dots under a week mean all seven days had reading; month labels carry hours read / heard.

daily reading and listening read listened 7 of 7 days

sample data Every number here is invented. All four variants read the same per-day fields: minutes read, pages, minutes heard.
