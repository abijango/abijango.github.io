Reading journal: three directions

Static design exploration for a future reading-year page. Pick the mood you like; nothing here is wired to anything.

sample data All titles, numbers and charts are invented. Covers are generated placeholders. No API, no Worker, no secrets. Not linked from the live site.

The reading log as another page of the existing site: monospace everything, hairline borders, blocky progress bars and a GitHub-style heat calendar. The most “already yours” option.

CAnnual report

A bold editorial take: a giant year, thick rules, numbered chapters, a cream paper band for goals and a pixel-per-day year chart. Loud covers on near-black, orange as the single hot accent.

palette
Ink, paper, Tokyo orange, lavender, pink
type
Fraunces 900 / 300 italic + JetBrains Mono labels
Main pageBook pageYear card

What each page has

  • Year header with switcher (2026, 2025, all time) and headline stats
  • Currently reading, yearly goals, finished-books wall
  • Titles per month, daily heat calendar, peak hours, favourite days, session length and pace
  • A per-book share page mock (try book-a.html?b=dune) and a 1200×630 year card

Notes

  • Plain HTML, CSS and JS. Shared example data and the cover generator live in assets/data.js.
  • Fonts: JetBrains Mono is bundled (OFL, same files as the site). Fraunces comes from Google Fonts, with Georgia as fallback.
  • Motion is limited to fades and bar, ring and chart fills, and is switched off under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Year card PNGs in assets/og-*.png are screenshots of the matching og-*.html.
